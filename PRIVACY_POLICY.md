eFootball Mini is a football game. This page explains what information the app
uses.

## Information stored on your device
The app saves these on your own device only: your player name and avatar, your
settings (sound, vibration, controls layout), your level, stats, achievements,
daily challenge progress, and your best match scores. This information stays on
your device. If you uninstall the app it is deleted.

## Online and local play
- **Internet play:** when you create or join an online room, your player name,
  avatar and the live game data (positions, score, button presses) are sent to
  a Firebase Realtime Database run by [som-dev team]. Room data is deleted
  when the host leaves the room.
- **Local play (no internet):** game data is sent directly between the phones
  on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. It is not sent to any server.

## Update check
When you open the app or tap "Check now" in Settings, the app contacts GitHub
to see whether a newer version is available. Only a standard internet request
is made. No personal information is sent. If you choose to update, the new
version is downloaded from GitHub.

## What we do not do
The app has no ads, no tracking and no analytics. We do not collect your
location, contacts, photos or any account details. We do not sell information.

## Children
The game is suitable for all ages. Players choose a nickname; please do not use
your real full name.
